    Research References — Formal Non-LLM Meaning Extraction¶

    Dokumen ini berisi referensi akademik untuk setiap jenis makna dan metode formal yang digunakan dalam desain 3 pathway.¶

    1. PRAGMATIK (Speaker Intent)¶

    Rational Speech Act (RSA) Model¶

    • Frank & Goodman (2012) "Predicting Pragmatic Reasoning in Language Games" Science 336(6084): 998
    • Goodman & Frank (2016) "Pragmatic Language Interpretation as Probabilistic Inference" Trends in Cognitive Sciences 20(11): 818-829
    • Degen (2023) "The Rational Speech Act Framework" Annual Review of Linguistics 9: 519-540
    • Scontras, Tessler & Franke (2021) "Probabilistic Language Understanding" (probLang textbook)

    Relevance Theory¶

    • Sperber & Wilson (1986/1995) Relevance: Communication and Cognition Blackwell
    • Wilson & Sperber (2004) "Relevance Theory" in The Handbook of Pragmatics Blackwell

    2. IMPLIKATUR (What's Implied But Not Said)¶

    Gricean Pragmatics¶

    • Grice, H.P. (1975) "Logic and Conversation" in Syntax and Semantics 3: Speech Acts pp. 41-58
    • Horn, L. (1984) "Toward a New Taxonomy for Pragmatic Inference" in Meaning, Form, and Use in Context Georgetown UP
    • Levinson, S. (2000) Presumptive Meanings: The Theory of Generalized Conversational Implicature MIT Press

    Scalar Implicature¶

    • Bar-Lev & Fox (2020) "Free choice, simplification, and innocent inclusion" Natural Language Semantics 28: 175-223
    • Chemla & Bott (2014) "Processing inferences at the semantics/pragmatics frontier" Cognition 130(3): 340-352

    3. PRESUPOSISI (What's Assumed)¶

    Dynamic Semantics¶

    • Heim, I. (1982) The Semantics of Definite and Indefinite Noun Phrases PhD thesis, UMass Amherst
    • Kamp, H. (1981) "A Theory of Truth and Semantic Representation" in Formal Methods in the Study of Language pp. 277-322
    • Groenendijk & Stokhof (1991) "Dynamic Predicate Logic" Linguistics and Philosophy 14: 39-100

    Accommodation¶

    • Stalnaker, R. (2002) "Common Ground" Linguistics and Philosophy 25: 701-721
    • Schlenker, P. (2009) "Local Contexts" Semantics and Pragmatics 2: 1-78
    • Beaver, D. (2001) Presupposition and Assertion in Dynamic Semantics CSLI Publications

    4. AFEKTIF (Emotional Charge)¶

    Appraisal Theory¶

    • Scherer, K. (1984) "On the Nature and Function of Emotion: A Component Process Approach" in Approaches to Emotion pp. 293-317
    • Scherer, K. (2001) "Appraisal Considered as a Process of Multilevel Sequential Checking" in Appraisal Processes in Emotion pp. 92-120
    • Lazarus, R. (1991) Emotion and Adaptation Oxford UP

    Computational Affect¶

    • Mohammad & Turney (2013) "Crowdsourcing a Word-Emotion Association Lexicon" Computational Intelligence 29(3): 436-465
    • Mohammad (2018) "Obtaining Reliable Human Ratings of Valence, Arousal, and Dominance for 20,000 English Words" ACL
    • Polanyi & Zaenen (2006) "Contextual Valence Shifters" in Computing Attitude and Affect in Text Springer
    • Warriner et al. (2013) "Norms of valence, arousal, and dominance for 13,915 English lemmas" Behavior Research Methods 45: 1191-1207

    5. SOSIAL (Power, Identity, Politeness)¶

    Politeness Theory¶

    • Brown & Levinson (1987) Politeness: Some Universals in Language Usage Cambridge UP
    • Terkourafi (2005) "Beyond the micro-level in politeness research" Journal of Politeness Research 1(2): 237-262

    Indexical Order¶

    • Silverstein, M. (2003) "Indexical Order and the Dialectics of Sociolinguistic Life" Language & Communication 23: 193-229
    • Eckert, P. (2008) "Variation and the Indexical Field" Journal of Sociolinguistics 12(4): 453-476

    6. KONOTATIF (Cultural Associations)¶

    Spreading Activation¶

    • Collins & Loftus (1975) "A Spreading-Activation Theory of Semantic Processing" Psychological Review 82(6): 407-428
    • Steyvers & Tenenbaum (2005) "The Large-Scale Structure of Semantic Networks" Cognitive Science 29(1): 41-78

    Cultural Networks¶

    • De Deyne et al. (2019) "The 'Small World of Words' English Word Association Norms" Behavior Research Methods 51: 987-1006
    • Sieck, Rasmussen & colleagues Cultural network analysis methodology

    7. PERFORMATIF (What Utterances DO)¶

    Speech Act Theory¶

    • Austin, J.L. (1962) How to Do Things with Words Oxford UP
    • Searle, J. (1969) Speech Acts: An Essay in the Philosophy of Language Cambridge UP
    • Searle & Vanderveken (1985) Foundations of Illocutionary Logic Cambridge UP

    Plan-Based Models¶

    • Cohen & Perrault (1979) "Elements of a Plan-Based Theory of Speech Acts" Cognitive Science 3: 177-212
    • Jurafsky et al. (1997) "Dialogue Act Modeling for Automatic Tagging and Recognition of Conversational Speech" ACL

    Computational¶

    • Lascarides & Asher (1993) "Default Logic and Speech Acts" SRI Technical Report

    Formal Semantics¶

    • Partee, B. (1984) "Compositionality" in Varieties of Formal Semantics pp. 281-311
    • Kamp & Reyle (1993) From Discourse to Logic Kluwer

    Type-Theoretic¶

    • Chatzikyriakidis & Luo (2020) "Formal Semantics in Modern Type Theories" Bulletin of the EATCS

    • Mann & Thompson (1988) "Rhetorical Structure Theory: Toward a Functional Theory of Text Organization" Text 8(3): 243-281
    • Howes & Meurers (2025) "eRST: A Signaled Graph Theory of Discourse Structure" Computational Linguistics

    Segmented DRT¶

    • Asher & Lascarides (2003) Logics of Conversation Cambridge UP

    Centering Theory¶

    • Grosz, Joshi & Weinstein (1995) "Centering: A Framework for Modeling the Local Coherence of Discourse" Computational Linguistics 21(2): 203-225

    Entity Grid¶

    • Barzilay & Lapata (2008) "Modeling Local Coherence: An Entity-Based Approach" Computational Linguistics 34(1): 1-34
    • Guinaudeau & Strube (2013) "Graph-based Local Coherence Modeling" ACL

    MRP 2020 Shared Task¶

    • Oepen et al. (2020) "MRP 2020: Cross-Framework Meaning Representation Parsing" CoNLL

    Unified Frameworks¶

    UMR / AMR Extensions¶

    • Van Gysel et al. (2021) "Designing a Uniform Meaning Representation for Natural Language Processing" K-CAP
    • Bonial et al. (2020) "Dialogue-AMR" LREC

    DRS as Graph¶

    • Oepen et al. (2020) MRP 2020 — DRS encoded as labeled directed graphs for computational parsing

    Key Open Problems¶

    1. Relevance Theory operationalization — No widely-accepted computational implementation. Cognitive effects / processing effort formalization remains qualitative.

    2. Unified pragmatics-semantics interface — RSA handles implicatures; SDRT handles discourse; Illocutionary Logic handles speech acts — no single formal system connects all three.

    3. Social meaning estimation from text alone — B&L D/P/R parameters require world knowledge not always in text.

    4. Composition rules for non-truth-conditional meaning — Appraisal, politeness, and connotative composition lack widely-accepted formal rules.

    5. Graph-native RSA — RSA is natively probabilistic; reformulating as graph computation requires message-passing on factor graphs or approximation via spreading activation.
